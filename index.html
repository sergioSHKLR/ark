Noah

The Protocol

The mental ark

The church in Rome under Nero could not vote the emperor out. They kept a lamp in a house, a marriage unpoisoned, and a mouth that did not run to every shrine of news. Noah did not drain the flood. He built through it.

You have a duty they did not: a ballot. Cast it when the day comes. Do not live in the campaign until then. You walk among a loud majority that talks like yours, and among kin who do not. The first is a temptation to perform. The second is the test of the house. This morning: pray, read, write — then go out.

Phone face down. Behind a door, not for men to see. If you are afraid, say so. Ask that this cup pass if it can, and that you will do the day’s duty if it will not. Ten honest minutes beat an hour in the groups.

One passage, drawn for this calendar day — scripture or a stoic older than the panic. Read it through. Copy a sentence if you must. That is the spine you carry into the street.

The rule of the day

You are a voter, not a correspondent. One sober act at the urn is not forty days of commentary. The country will be decided by millions of strangers. This house will be decided by whether you showed up as a husband, and whether a relative still had a table to sit at.

Desk

Latin as texture while you work. No English, no Portuguese, no drums. Local files replace YouTube when you add them.

Chant at rest

The election does not need a dawn briefing. Mute the groups. Leave the pundits closed. If you must know a date, look it up once. What the crowd cheers — even the crowd that agrees with you — is not your food.

The harder silence is not with strangers. It is with kin who already know where you stand. A vocal majority makes the next remark feel like courage. It is usually only noise. You will not evangelize an uncle at lunch. Courtesy is not surrender.

She does not need a digest of the race. She needs you unhooked from the groups. Name the strain. Agree that this house is not a rally, and that neither of you will use the other as a weapon at lunch.

Sunday lunch will try to become a debate. Let it fail. Pass the rice. Be briefly dull. You may love people who will mark a different ballot. A joke that is only a knife is still a knife.

The fire that wants one more audio is the Forum wearing your face. Spend it on the body, a skill, a page, the partnership. Be tired tonight from what you kept, not from what you forwarded.

Close the day

Close as they closed it: a few true sentences, then quiet. Did you keep the house? Did you leave a relative in peace? Release the argument you still want to win. Tomorrow the urns and the groups will still be there. You do not have to sleep inside them.

The log

Not a score. Proof that you kept showing up — days on the ark, words you actually wrote, a streak only you can see.
